# KASAUTI — research and positioning docs

Background research for KASAUTI, an entry to the Meta Global AI Developer
Hackathon (Indie Developer track, solo). Submission due 4 Nov 2026, 9:00 PM ET.

Research conducted 26 Oct 2026. Every factual claim here is dated and sourced;
anything that is an inference rather than a sourced fact is labelled as such in
the text. Treat undated claims as unverified.

## Contents

- `feasibility-and-positioning.md` — the synthesis. Read this first.
  Covers: SEBI data accessibility, published evidence on Meta models'
  Indic-language capability, competitive positioning against the judging
  criteria, the sourced impact evidence pack, risks, and recommended build order.

- `research-notes/sebi_registry_access.md` — how (and whether) SEBI
  registration and enforcement data can be accessed programmatically.
  Exact endpoints, formats, record counts, legal position.

- `research-notes/meta_model_api_capabilities.md` — Meta Model API reference:
  per-model modalities, pricing, rate limits, documented language support,
  platform constraints.

- `research-notes/finfluencer_harm_evidence.md` — quantified, dated evidence on
  financial misinformation and retail investor harm in India.

- `research-notes/prior_art_and_methods.md` — existing products and literature,
  plus the conformal prediction method notes (calibration sizing, abstention
  mechanisms, reporting metrics).

## Two claims deliberately NOT made

1. KASAUTI does not assess whether a tip is *true*. It assesses whether the
   content constitutes a regulated act and whether required disclosures are
   present.
2. No source causally links finfluencer content to SEBI's documented F&O
   trading losses. Those figures size retail harm; they do not attribute it.
